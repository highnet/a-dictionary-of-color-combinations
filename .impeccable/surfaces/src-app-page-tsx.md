## Direction contract

THESIS: The archive is a printer's proof room. Each combination is pulled as a woodblock proof: flat, unmodulated blocks of exact colour registered on washi by kentō marks. Refuses the category default of a swatch-card grid on a white SaaS page and the cream-serif "editorial" cliché.
OWN-WORLD: Kozo washi ground (cool unbleached, faint fibre), sumi ink for type and key-lines, shu vermilion only for the vertical plate cartouche. Colour fields own whole regions; texture never touches a swatch. Kentō corner (kagi) + side bar (hikitsuke) frame every plate. Bokashi graded band where a palette meets the paper. Sumi-ground interlude sections.
STORY: Visitor sees a living woodblock wave printed in one Wada combination, reads what the dictionary is, searches a number/HEX or opens the archive, lands on a plate and copies or exports its values.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 5/12: Mincho title at display scale, one-line credit to Sanzo Wada, a working search field (number or HEX) and "Open the archive". Right 7/12 full-bleed: WebGL wave in flat bands of the current plate's colours with sumi contour lines, bokashi sky; a vermilion vertical cartouche reads the plate number and links to it; swatch chips with HEX beneath.
FORM: woodblock colour proof (ukiyo-e print technique), grounded list position 3; seed:6f757b00e1309214. Raises: state is a mark, never a hue (cutting-bench); the archive is a packed wall of plates (video-store density); the plate count is the only ornament (exposed binding).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
